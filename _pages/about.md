I am currently an Associate Professor in the School of Software Engineering at South China University of Technology. Prior to that, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, working with Prof. Ju Ren and Prof. Fuchun Sun. I received my Ph.D. from Central South University, advised by Prof. Feng Lyu and Prof. Zhigang Chen. During my Ph.D, I was a visiting scholar at McGill University, under the supervision of Prof. Steve Xue Liu (Fellow of CAE, IEEE) and Prof. Desheng Zhang.

My research concentrates on distributed embodied AI, edge intelligence, data science in human-centric cyber-physical systems, and LLM agent. I am actively looking for self-motivated students. If you are interested, please drop me an email with your CV.
