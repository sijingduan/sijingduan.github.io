I am currently an Associate Professor in the <span style="color: #b000b5;">School of Software Engineering at South China University of Technology</span>. Prior to that, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the <span style="color: #b000b5;">Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University</span>, working with Prof. <a href="https://juren1987.github.io/" style="color: #b000b5;">Ju Ren</a> and Prof. <a href="https://www.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1121/3555.htm" style="color: #b000b5;">Fuchun Sun</a>. I received my Ph.D. from <span style="color: #b000b5;">Central South University</span>, advised by Prof. <a href="https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/fenglyu/zh_CN/index.htm" style="color: #b000b5;">Feng Lyu</a> and Prof. <a href="https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/chenzhigang/zh_CN/index.htm" style="color: #b000b5;">Zhigang Chen</a>. During my Ph.D., I was a visiting scholar at <span style="color: #b000b5;">McGill University</span>, under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://cs.mcgill.ca/~xueliu/site/intro.html" style="color: #b000b5;">Xue (Steve) Liu</a> (Fellow of CAE, IEEE) and Prof. <a href="https://people.cs.rutgers.edu/~dz220/" style="color: #b000b5;">Desheng Zhang</a>. My research concentrates on distributed embodied AI, edge intelligence, data science in human-centric cyber-physical systems, and LLM agent.
