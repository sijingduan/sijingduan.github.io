I am Sijing Duan. My research spans mobile edge computing, distributed artificial intelligence, and data-driven networking. I study how computing and learning can be coordinated across devices, edge servers, and the cloud, with applications in mobile networks and logistics.

I received my Ph.D. from Central South University. My publications are listed on the [Publications page](/publications/) and on Google Scholar.
